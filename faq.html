Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about joining, donating and our programs

How do I join as a volunteer?

Fill in the volunteer form on the Get Involved page. We will contact you about suitable opportunities.

How can I donate?

Contact us through the Contact page and we will share M-Pesa, bank and other payment options.

Can my company partner with you?

Yes. We work with corporates, grant-makers and institutions. See the Partnership section on Get Involved.

Where do you work?

Across Kenya and East Africa, including Nakuru, Kisumu, Garissa, Nyeri, Busia, Machakos and Turkana.

Do you offer membership?

Yes. Send us a message with the subject Membership and we will explain how to join.

How are donations used?

Funds go to program delivery such as seedlings, training, school supplies and sanitary products, with regular reporting.

Dedicated to promoting environmental conservation, sustainable development, and community empowerment across Kenya and East Africa.
