About Eagle Ecovision

Building sustainable communities across Kenya and East Africa

Our Story

Eagle Ecovision CBO is a community-based organization working in Kenya and East Africa. We began with a simple belief: communities that understand their land, water, energy and education options can build their own futures. Today we train farmers, plant trees, distribute learning and hygiene supplies, and support women, youth, orphans and children. Edit this paragraph with your founding year and story.

Our Mission

To foster sustainable development through environmental conservation, renewable energy, sustainable agriculture, and quality education while empowering vulnerable groups including women, orphans, youth, and children through comprehensive training, resource provision, and community-driven programs.

Our Vision

A thriving East Africa where communities are empowered with sustainable practices, environmental stewardship is a way of life, and every individual, regardless of gender or background, has access to opportunities for growth, education, and a dignified life.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do.

Inclusivity
