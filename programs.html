Our Programs

Comprehensive initiatives driving sustainable development across East Africa

Creating Lasting Impact

Our integrated programs address the connected challenges facing communities in Kenya and East Africa.

Sustainable Agriculture

Growing food security and prosperity

Climate-smart farming, modern practices and organic methods for farmers.

  • Climate-smart farming training
  • Quality seeds and farming tools
  • Organic farming and composting workshops
  • Farmer cooperatives and market linkage

Impact: Over 500 farmers trained, with a 40% increase in crop yields.

Renewable Energy

Powering communities sustainably

Solar, biogas and energy-efficient technologies that cut carbon and improve daily life.

  • Solar installation and maintenance training
  • Biogas digester workshops
  • Energy-efficient cooking solutions
  • Household and institutional energy audits

Impact: 200+ households moved to clean energy, saving 60 tons of carbon yearly.

Water & Sanitation

Health and dignity for all

Clean water, sanitation facilities and hygiene education, with focus on girls' education.

  • Clean water projects
  • School sanitation facilities
  • Sanitary towels for girls
  • Rainwater harvesting systems

Impact: 1,200+ girls supported; school attendance up 35%.

Environmental Conservation

Protecting our planet

Tree planting, waste management and environmental education to restore ecosystems.

  • Community tree planting campaigns
  • Waste management and recycling
  • Water catchment conservation
  • Climate change adaptation training

Impact: 10,000+ trees planted across 15 communities.

Education Support

Empowering minds

Learning materials and resources so every child can access quality education.

  • Textbooks and learning materials
  • School supply drives
  • Scholarships and rural libraries
  • Mentorship and tutoring

Impact: 800+ children received learning materials.

Community Empowerment

Stronger, self-reliant communities

Skills training, resources and mentorship for women, youth, orphans and children.

  • Vocational skills training
  • Support for orphans and vulnerable children
  • Savings and credit groups
  • Leadership and entrepreneurship programs

Impact: 300+ women and youth trained; 70% started their own businesses.
