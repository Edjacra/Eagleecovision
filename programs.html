Our Core Pillars & Programs

Grassroots youth-led initiatives driving good governance, environmental conservation, education support, and community empowerment across Kisumu County

Strategic Focus Areas

Good governance and accountability form the foundation of Eagle Eco-Vision’s work. The other three focus areas translate accountable leadership and meaningful community participation into practical, lasting development outcomes.

FOUNDATIONAL PILLAR

Good Governance & Accountability

Promoting civic education, transparency, responsive leadership, public participation, and community-led accountability

Good governance and accountability form the foundation of Eagle Eco-Vision’s work. We actively empower local communities to know their rights, participate in public decision-making barazas, demand transparency, and advocate for responsive leadership and community-led accountability across Kisumu County.

  • Civic education barazas & public policy engagement
  • Legal rights, transparency & responsive leadership forums
  • Youth-led civic advocacy & social accountability monitoring
  • Community-driven governance & public participation dialogues
  • Social accountability & transparent resource tracking

Impact: Over 45 civic governance barazas convened, training 600+ community advocacy leaders across Kisumu County.

Environmental Conservation

Mobilizing communities, especially young people, to protect natural resources and adopt sustainable environmental practices

We mobilize communities, especially young people, to protect natural resources, restore degraded ecosystems, plant indigenous trees, and adopt sustainable environmental stewardship practices.

  • Youth-led community and school tree planting drives
  • Waste sorting, recycling, and plastic reduction campaigns
  • Natural resource protection & riparian zone conservation
  • Climate change adaptation & environmental resilience education

Impact: 10,000+ indigenous trees planted and nurtured across rural and peri-urban communities.

Education Support

Promoting equitable access to learning opportunities through educational support, mentorship, and initiatives that help learners overcome barriers

We promote equitable access to learning opportunities through educational support, mentorship, and targeted initiatives that help underprivileged children and youth overcome barriers and reach their full potential.

  • Textbooks, exercise books, and essential school supplies distribution
  • Student mentorship and academic retention programs
  • Initiatives to help learners overcome social and economic barriers
  • Civic learning, after-school clubs, and literacy support

Impact: 800+ underprivileged children equipped with essential learning materials and mentorship.

Community Empowerment

Building the capacity of grassroots communities through youth leadership, skills development, inclusive participation, and locally led solutions

We build the capacity of grassroots communities through youth leadership, skills development, inclusive participation, and locally led solutions that create sustainable livelihoods and self-reliance.

  • Youth leadership development and civic capacity workshops
  • Vocational and technical skills development for grassroots youth and women
  • Inclusive community participation in local development decisions
  • Support for locally led community enterprises and development solutions

Impact: 400+ youth and women trained in leadership, entrepreneurship, and community development.
