What We Do

Our core pillars, strategic focus areas, and community objectives

Strategic Focus Areas

Good governance and accountability form the foundation of Eagle Eco-Vision’s work. The other three focus areas translate accountable leadership and meaningful community participation into practical, lasting development outcomes.

FOUNDATIONAL PILLAR

Good Governance & Accountability

Promoting civic education, transparency, responsive leadership, public participation, and community-led accountability.

Environmental Conservation

Mobilizing communities, especially young people, to protect natural resources and adopt sustainable environmental practices.

Education Support

Promoting equitable access to learning opportunities through educational support, mentorship, and initiatives that help learners overcome barriers.

Community Empowerment

Building the capacity of grassroots communities through youth leadership, skills development, inclusive participation, and locally led solutions.

Our Core Activities

Civic Engagement & Youth Leadership

  • Good Governance & Accountability barazas for community rights
  • Civic education and grassroots public participation dialogues
  • Youth-led environmental conservation and tree planting drives
  • Student mentorship and educational barrier-reduction workshops
  • Vocational skills development and youth leadership training

Grassroots & Community Support

  • Community policy advocacy and transparent resource monitoring
  • Educational supplies and learning materials for underprivileged students
  • Mentorship programs to help learners stay in school and excel
  • Indigenous tree seedlings distribution and environmental restoration
  • Grassroots capacity building and community-led development solutions

Eagle Ecovision is dedicated to promoting advocacy, environmental conservation, good governance, sustainable development, and community empowerment across Kenya and East Africa.
